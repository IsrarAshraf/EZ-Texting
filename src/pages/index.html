COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What the FAQ?

  • What is Text Marketing?
    Text marketing stands for short message service marketing and operates via texting apps such as Google Messages and iMessages. You may know text marketing as SMS marketing, or possibly mass texting or bulk texting, but they all refer to the same thing — a modern, mobile channel that allows businesses and marketers to reach their customers and clients faster and more effectively than ever. Its most basic usage is to send notifications, alerts (inclement weather or itinerary changes), appointment and delivery reminders, and to send mass text messages that include promotions and exclusive offers, but that’s just the tip of the iceberg.
  • Does Text Marketing Really Work?
    Absolutely.
    What other channel offers a 98% engagement rate with most messages being read within minutes (or seconds) of receipt? People love to text and we've seen truly impactful numbers for our customers, including CTRs at 40% and four-figure ROI for some customers. But not all mass texting services are created equal, so be sure to look for the SMS features you'll need, like automations or list growth tools to best reach your target audience. EZ Texting is mass texting service that offers both, along with dozens of other vital SMS features designed to help you win.
